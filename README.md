Vice Cover Studio is a little poster workshop I built for turning everyday photos into bold, multi-panel game-cover art. You load one photo or several, and the app slices them across diagonal poster layouts — a four-panel Classic Wedge, a Cover Story layout with one big hero panel, a Six Pack grid, or a simple Duo split. Every panel is its own frame: click it, drag to reframe the photo inside it, zoom with the slider or the mouse wheel, swap which photo it shows, or stretch a single photo across all the panels at once. Five colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour, Noir and Clean) wash over the whole composition, my own canvas-drawn sun and palm silhouettes finish the scene, and the title is stamped on top in heavy skewed lettering with a subtitle bar underneath. When it looks right, one click exports a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. The app is plain static files with no build step and no network calls, so it works fully offline and your photos never leave the browser. Three sample photos are included so you can make a cover in seconds; all layouts, lettering and decoration are original work made for this project.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser, drop in a photo, and export your cover.
